# Earnings: legit ways to get paid for GTA clips

Rates in the campaign examples below were checked on 19 November 2026. Use the current campaign brief and listed budget when deciding whether a clip qualifies.

## Connect Whop

1. Open your Whop business dashboard and create an Account API Key under **Developer** with the `bounty:basic:read` permission. Copy the `biz_` account ID from the dashboard address.
2. Under **Developer → Apps**, create an app and open **OAuth**. Register `http://localhost:8788/whop/oauth/callback` with your configured port. Enable `oauth:token_exchange` on the app’s Permissions page and copy the OAuth client ID and secret.
3. Open Clip Desk Setup using `http://localhost:8788/setup#whop`. Save the keys, account ID and your country code. Click **Connect Whop user** and grant access on Whop.
4. In **Earnings**, scan the visible bounties. For a campaign you intend to work on, check its conditions and footage rights, then enable the brief with its creators, duration, view threshold, post age, hashtags and disclosure.

The worker scans about every 45 minutes. It attempts up to three submissions per South African calendar day when confirmed TikTok uploads meet enabled briefs. It rechecks Whop before each attempt and tracks the submitted URL and provider receipt. Listed rewards are estimates based on the bounty. The campaign and Whop determine approval and payment.

Content Rewards and other campaign boards use their own application and verification workflows. Their leads remain accessible from Earnings. ## How this fits the tool
- The Earnings tab marks a potential match when a campaign names the streamer. Confirm the campaign gives you the rights to use that footage and follow its brief.
- Use **My submissions** to log work sent through other campaign platforms.
- Never buy views, never pay to join, and keep posts public until you've been paid.

**Four questions to ask before joining any campaign:** (1) What is the max payout per clip? (2) If the budget runs out, do clips you've already posted still pay? (3) Are the rules written down before you post? (4) When are approval and cash-out? Pick two boards in one niche, and submit Content Rewards links fast (within about 30 minutes).
